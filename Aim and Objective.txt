AIM AND OBJECTIVE

Aim
To design and build a role-based web platform that makes fuel availability visible and fuel collection more orderly for drivers, station staff, fuel companies, and the platform owner.

Objectives
- Provide a public homepage with fuel prices, news, and station information.
- Let verified drivers book fuel at a chosen station and receive a QR code that is valid for a limited time.
- Let station staff verify bookings and record the fuel actually dispensed.
- Apply fair-use rules, including one active booking per driver, a weekly litre quota, and booking expiry.
- Give branch managers tools to manage staff, fuel stock, deliveries, and booking slots.
- Give fuel company headquarters tools to manage stations, prices, manager reports, and billing records.
- Give moderators and the admin tools to review documents, manage accounts, handle requests, and audit activity.
- Reward drivers through a configurable loyalty-points system.
